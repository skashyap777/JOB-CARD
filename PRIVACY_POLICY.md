# Privacy Policy for Smart Job Card

**Last Updated: May 05, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Job Card ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Permissions
To provide our services, the App requires certain permissions on your mobile device:

*   **Camera:** We use the camera permission to allow you to scan QR codes on job cards. This is necessary to quickly retrieve job card details without manual entry.
*   **Internet Access:** We require internet access to communicate with our servers and fetch the latest job card information.
*   **Network State:** We check your network state to ensure you have an active connection before attempting to fetch data.
*   **Storage:** We use local storage (Shared Preferences) to save your job card details on your device. This allows you to access your information quickly without re-scanning or re-searching every time you open the App.

### 1.2 Data Collected Automatically
When you use the App, we may collect certain information automatically, including:
*   Your device type and operating system version.
*   App usage statistics (e.g., how many times the App is opened).
*   IP address (collected by our servers during API requests).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
*   Provide and maintain the App's functionality.
*   Retrieve and display job card information from the official portal (`jobcard.h24x7.in`).
*   Improve the user experience and App performance.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:
*   **Service Providers:** We may use third-party plugins (like `mobile_scanner`) that help us provide the App's features. These third parties have access to your information only to perform these tasks on our behalf.
*   **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

## 4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We implement standard security measures to protect the information you provide through the App. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to or fetch data from third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of these third-party services. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party service you visit.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
